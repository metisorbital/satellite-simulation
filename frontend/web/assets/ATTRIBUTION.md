# Local visual assets

- `earth_atmos_2048.jpg`: Earth texture distributed in three.js r180, retrieved from <https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/blob/r180/examples/textures/planets/earth_atmos_2048.jpg>. three.js is MIT licensed, copyright 2010–2025 three.js authors. The imagery is illustrative and is not a current weather observation.
- The CesiumJS 1.145.0 distribution supplies local skybox, widget, and rendering assets. CesiumJS is Apache-2.0 licensed. Its source credits remain visible in the viewer.
- `metis-mark.svg`: original geometric application mark.
- `stars.svg`: original decorative starfield; not a stellar catalog or an attitude reference.

All visual assets are served locally. The viewer makes no Cesium ion, map-provider, or online image requests.
